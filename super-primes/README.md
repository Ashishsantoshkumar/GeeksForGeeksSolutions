<h2><a href="https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/super-primes2443/1">Super Primes</a></h2><h3>Medium</h3><hr><p><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Given a positive integer <strong>n</strong>, return all super-prime numbers less than or equal to n.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 14pt;">A super-prime is a prime number whose position in the sequence of prime numbers is also prime.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Examples:</strong></span></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>n = 5
<strong>Output:</strong> [3, 5]
<strong>Explanation: </strong>The prime numbers up to 5 are [2, 3, 5]. Their positions are [1, 2, 3]. Since positions 2 and 3 are prime, 3 and 5 are super-primes.</span></pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>n = 20 
<strong>Output:</strong> [3, 5, 11, 17]
<strong>Explanation: </strong>The prime numbers up to 20 are [2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19]. Their positions are [1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8]. Since positions 2, 3, 5, and 7 are prime, 3, 5, 11, and 17 are super-primes.</span></pre>
